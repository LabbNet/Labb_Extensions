Labb rapid point-of-care tests (POCTs) ship with a 24-month expiration date. When a lot reaches expiration, its shelf life can be extended by running quality controls. Each passing control extends the lot's expiration to 60 days after the control date, and controls can be repeated to extend a lot for up to one additional year beyond its original expiration.
